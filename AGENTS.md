## Pull requests, issues and comments

The body of anything an agent posts to GitHub, Jira or a mailing list (PR, issue, review, comment) starts with a quote line naming the model and harness, and the human it acts for:

```
> [Claude Opus 5.5][Claude Code] on behalf of <name>.
```

Jira uses `bq.` instead of `>`. Titles and commit subjects keep their usual format.
