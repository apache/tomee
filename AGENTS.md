## Pull requests, issues and comments

The body of anything an agent posts to GitHub, Jira or a mailing list (PR, issue, review, comment) starts with a note naming the model and the human it acts for:

```
> [!NOTE]
> 🤖 **Claude Opus 5.5 responding on behalf of <name>**
```

Pick the verb that fits (`writing` for a PR or issue, `responding` for a comment or review). Jira and mailing lists take the line without the note markup (Jira bolds with `*…*`). Titles and commit subjects keep their usual format.
